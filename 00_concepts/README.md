# Concepts

**What:** The ideas and vocabulary behind agentic AI — no implementation, just
notebooks working through the reasoning.

**Why:** Before writing runtime code, it's worth understanding what's actually
being built and why existing patterns (ReAct, Plan & Execute, ToT, Reflexion,
Debate, ...) look the way they do — as compositions of a small set of
cognitive primitives, rather than as unrelated named techniques.

## Notebooks

- `03_design_patterns.ipynb` — deriving agentic design patterns from first
  principles: Observe / Think / Act as the cognitive primitives, and how
  known patterns (ReAct, Plan & Execute, Reflection, ToT, ReWoo, Debate)
  emerge as different compositions of them.

(`01_agentic_ai_vs_workflows` and `02_agent_types` are planned but not
written yet.)
